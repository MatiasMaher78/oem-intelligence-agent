01 / THE BUSINESS PROBLEM

Old stock is a signal. Not a verdict.

In automotive dismantling, a slow-moving part can have a correct OEM and reasonable pricing. Other listings may look similar but refer to an incompatible version. The goal is to separate facts from assumptions before a human makes a decision.

01 / OEM

Uncertain identification

Part numbers must match exactly. Similar-looking codes are not proof of identity.

02 / MARKET

Misleading comparisons

Listings must be checked for declared compatibility, price validity, stock and source coverage.

03 / OPERATIONS

Decisions without context

A number alone cannot justify repricing. Reviewers need explainable evidence and clear limits.

Business rules determine what to investigate. Deterministic processing builds the evidence. AI helps explain it; people retain decision authority.

03 / HOW IT WAS BUILT

Deterministic data. Controlled AI.

The LLM does not calculate the market statistics or issue arbitrary SQL. It selects from five read-only tools over previously prepared evidence.

02

03
LLM + local tool harnessStrict arguments, read-only allowlist, pagination and budgets
04
